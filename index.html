How it is calculated
  • Dry volume = wet volume × 1.54 (accounts for voids & bulking).
  • Cement = dry volume × (cement part ÷ sum of ratio) × 1440 kg/m³ ÷ 50 kg per bag.
  • Sand / aggregate = dry volume × (part ÷ sum of ratio) × 35.3147 cft/m³.
  • Water = cement (kg) × water-cement ratio (M25 0.45, M20 0.50, M15 0.55, M10 0.60).

How it is calculated
  • Main bars run along the shorter span; number = (longer span − 2 × cover) ÷ spacing + 1.
  • Bars along the longer span: number = (shorter span − 2 × cover) ÷ spacing + 1. They are distribution bars in a one-way slab and main bars in a two-way slab.
  • Cutting length = span − 2 × cover + extra length. Cranked bars add 2 × 0.42 D (D = slab thickness − 2 × cover − bar dia).
  • Weight = total length × d²/162 kg/m.

How it is calculated
  • Main bar length = beam length − 2 × cover + extra length (anchorage / development length).
  • Stirrup cutting length = 2(a + b) + 2 × 10d (135° hooks) − 3 × 2d (90° bends) − 2 × 3d (135° bends), where a = B − 2c − d and b = D − 2c − d.
  • Number of stirrups = (length − 2 × cover) ÷ spacing + 1.

How it is calculated
  • Vertical bar length = column height + extra length (lap / footing anchorage).
  • Rectangular tie = 2(a + b) + 8d, with a = width − 2c − d, b = length − 2c − d.
  • Circular tie = π × (D − 2c − d) + 14d.
  • Number of ties = height ÷ spacing + 1. Steel % = area of bars ÷ column area × 100 (IS 456 limits: 0.8 % – 4 %).

Project details
Letterhead (all projects)
How it is calculated
  • Quantity of a row = Nos × L × W × H. Leave a dimension empty to skip it: L × H gives the area of a wall, L × W × D gives a volume.
  • Type a value in Qty to enter the quantity directly (e.g. 2800 sq ft).
  • Rows marked − are deductions (doors, windows, ventilators) and are subtracted from the total.
  • Amount = net quantity × rate. A lump-sum (LS) item adds its amount as it is.
  • Total = sum of all amounts (+ GST if entered). Projects are saved on this device; use Save file to back up or send a project.
